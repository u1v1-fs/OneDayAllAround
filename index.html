Help & support

About this project

FaHaM is a single-page 24-hour clock and Islamic daily-remembrance companion. It brings prayer-time context, Qibla direction, fasting reminders, Quran reflections, Prophetic teachings, and optional recitation routines into one responsive page.

Clock, location, and prayer times

The clock follows your device's local time by default. Location is used for prayer-time and daylight calculations; prayer calculation method, adjustments, and manual timings can be changed in Settings. Device location requires permission and a secure page. You can enter latitude and longitude manually when location access is unavailable.

Qibla Finder

The bearing is calculated from the selected coordinates to the Kaaba using the great-circle bearing, measured clockwise from true north. Choose Enable live compass and grant motion permission when your browser asks; keep the phone flat and turn slowly. The compass needle then points toward the Qibla relative to the phone's direction. If live heading is unsupported or permission is denied, use the displayed true-north bearing with a physical compass. Device sensors can be affected by nearby metal, magnets, and calibration; compare with a trusted local Qibla source if readings seem unstable.

Quran and Prophetic reflections

The two reflection cards can show a fresh Quran verse and a curated Prophetic narration. Use the refresh arrow for an immediate change, or leave Auto refresh - 2 min enabled. Previously shown material is tracked on this device to avoid repeats. Once all currently available unique narrations have been seen, the app reports that no unseen item remains rather than repeating a hadith. Dynamic Quran retrieval and translation/audio links need an internet connection.

Remembrance and recitation

Duas include Quranic supplications and everyday duas with references. Arabic dua playback uses the device's speech-synthesis voice; it is not a recorded reciter. Quran recitations are streamed ayah-by-ayah from EveryAyah, so audio requires internet access. Time-specific suggestions are separate from optional personal routines; choose By time or Show all in Settings.

Fasting reminders and privacy

Fasting reminders are calculated locally from the selected Hijri date and preferences. Browser notifications require permission; if unavailable, time-based and fasting reminders appear inside the app while its page remains open. Preferences and seen-item history are stored in this browser's local storage; location is used in the page for calculations and is not sent to a project account.

Troubleshooting

  • For location and live compass, open the app on HTTPS or localhost and allow the relevant browser permission. If location is blocked, change Location to Allow in the browser address-bar site controls and retry.
  • If the Qibla arrow is not tracking, tap Enable live compass, rotate the phone slowly, check the device's compass calibration, and move away from magnetic covers or metal objects.
  • If a reflection does not change, check the visible countdown and connection. Audio playback temporarily delays automatic changes; use the refresh arrow for an immediate request.
  • If audio is unavailable, check your connection; for Arabic dua speech, install or enable an Arabic voice in your device settings.

Sources are linked beside each reflection and recitation. For questions about the app, use the project conversation where this HTML was provided.
